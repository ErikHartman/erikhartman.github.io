Input formats

Use a column named peptide or sequence. Add abundance and sample columns for long-format data, or give each sample its own abundance column.

peptide,Control,Treatment
WVTFISLL,420,180
LLFLFSS,80,240

FASTA and plain-text peptide lists are also accepted. Blank abundances in a sample column mean that the peptide was unobserved in that sample.
